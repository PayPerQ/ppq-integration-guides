## What is RxResume?

RxResume (Reactive Resume) is a powerful, free and open-source resume builder that simplifies creating, updating, and sharing professional resumes. With built-in AI integration, 12 customizable templates, and multi-language support, it's trusted by professionals landing jobs at companies like Amazon, Google, and Twilio.

Key features include:
- **Completely Free**: No hidden costs or premium tiers
- **Open Source**: Full transparency and community-driven development
- **AI Integration**: Built-in OpenAI compatibility for content enhancement
- **12 Professional Templates**: From minimalist to creative designs
- **Multi-language Support**: Available in 50+ languages
- **Privacy Focused**: Self-hostable with secure authentication
- **Real-time Collaboration**: Share and track resume views/downloads

## Step by Step Set Up

### 1. Access RxResume

Go to [rxresu.me](https://rxresu.me) and create your free account using GitHub, Google, or email authentication.

### 2. Create or Import Your Resume

Start with a template or import your existing resume data. RxResume supports various formats and provides intuitive editing tools.

### 3. Enable AI Integration

In your resume settings, navigate to the AI integration section and configure it to use OpenAI-compatible endpoints.

![AI Setup](/blog-images/using-rxresume-with-payperq/setup_screenshot.png)

### 4. Configure PayPerQ Integration

- **API Endpoint**: Set to `https://api.ppq.ai`
- **API Key**: Enter your PayPerQ API key from [ppq.ai/api-docs](https://ppq.ai/api-docs)
- **Model Selection**: Choose your preferred model for content enhancement

## Enhancing Your Resume with PayPerQ Models

Once connected, leverage PayPerQ's diverse model selection for different resume sections:

- **Professional Summary**: Use Claude for sophisticated, professional tone
- **Work Experience**: Use GPT-4 for detailed accomplishment descriptions
- **Skills Section**: Use specialized models to match industry keywords
- **Cover Letters**: Generate tailored content for specific positions

Simply select text in any resume section and use the AI assistant to refine, expand, or rewrite content based on your chosen PayPerQ model.

## Advanced Tips

- **Industry-Specific Optimization**: Switch between models based on your target industry
- **ATS Optimization**: Use AI to incorporate relevant keywords for applicant tracking systems
- **Multiple Versions**: Create industry-specific resume variants using different AI models
- **Content Refinement**: Iterate on sections using various models for the best results
