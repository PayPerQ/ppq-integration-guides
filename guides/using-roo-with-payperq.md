## What is Roo?

Roo is an intelligent VS Code extension that transforms your coding experience by providing AI-powered pair programming capabilities directly within your editor. Roo is a fork of Cline, building upon its foundation to deliver enhanced AI assistance. Unlike traditional AI tools, Roo integrates seamlessly with your development workflow to provide contextual code suggestions, refactoring assistance, and architectural guidance.
